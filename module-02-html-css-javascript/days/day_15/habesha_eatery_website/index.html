OUR STORY

Welcome to Habesha Eatery

Habesha Eatery brings the rich flavors and traditions of Ethiopian cuisine to the heart of Addis Ababa.

From freshly prepared injera to delicious wot and tibs, every dish is made with carefully selected ingredients and traditional spices.

Whether you're enjoying a family meal, meeting friends, or celebrating a special occasion, we welcome you to experience Ethiopian hospitality.
